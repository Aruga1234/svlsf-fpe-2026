About the Programme

A structured platform for recognizing professional excellence through institutional nominations

Executive Summary

The Sri Vara Laxmi Sridhar Foundation Fellowship in Professional Excellence is a proposed institutional recognition and certification programme intended to identify, recognize and certify professionals, faculty members, researchers, administrators, students and other eligible individuals who demonstrate excellence, leadership, innovation, service, academic contribution or professional achievement.

Nominations are invited from Principals, Directors, Deans and Heads of Institutions across Engineering, Pharmacy, Medical, Dental, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences and related fields.

Background & Rationale

Professional education is a vital contributor to India’s scientific, technological, healthcare and socioeconomic development. Despite significant contributions made by many professionals, there is a need for structured institutional mechanisms through which outstanding contributions can be formally recognized.

This Fellowship creates a credible platform for institutions to nominate deserving individuals and foster a culture of excellence, innovation, professional ethics, research and leadership.

Fellowship Categories

Eight specialized categories to recognize diverse forms of excellence

Academic Excellence

Outstanding contributions to teaching, education, mentoring and academic development.

Research Excellence

Substantial research contributions, publications, projects, patents or scientific work.

Professional Excellence

Distinguished professional achievement and contribution to the profession.

Innovation & Technology

Technological innovation, product development or technology-driven solutions.

Healthcare Excellence

Contributions to medical, pharmacy, nursing, allied health or healthcare services.

Institutional Leadership

Significant contributions to institutional development, administration or leadership.

Community Service

Documented contributions to education, healthcare, humanitarian or social welfare.

Emerging Excellence

Early-career professionals, researchers or young academics showing significant potential.

Eligibility & Evaluation

Eligibility Framework

  • Associated with a recognized institution or professional organization
  • Documented professional, academic, research, innovation or service achievements
  • Record that can reasonably be evaluated by the Foundation
  • Accurate information and supporting documentation
  • Satisfy specific requirements of the applicable fellowship category

  • Completed nomination form
  • Nominee’s CV / Profile
  • Institutional recommendation
  • Description of achievements
  • Publications, patents, projects (if applicable)
  • Awards & recognition details
  • Community-service documentation
  • Identity & professional credentials
